\section{Broader Impact}
\label{sec:part3}
% Four ledger-grounded reflections. No generic manifesto.

Guardrails earned their keep when they failed \emph{closed}.
The BIRD checker aborted on a missing \texttt{sha256} key rather
than packing a substitute hash or writing a fake
\texttt{metrics.json}; the later CUDA OOM likewise produced no
label.
% ledger 2026-08-24 KeyError; 2026-08-25 S2 OOM; AR.md L27--29
GSM8K's negative $\Delta_{\mathrm{full}}$ came with unparseable
$0/200$ and a format-compliance shift from mixed
\texttt{\#\#\#\#}/last-number at base to \texttt{\#\#\#\#} 200/200
after training --- evidence against an EOS/truncation story, and
the reason the operator could rule ``format overwrite'' instead of
discarding a negative as a generation glitch.
% GATE_REPORT L21, L28, L32; AR.md L58
A rail that invents a number to keep a queue green is not a rail.

Pre-registration is what made the negative primary endpoint
publishable. The honesty clause (bootstrap CI covering $0.5$
equals a negative, to be printed) was committed before the
remaining sixty GPU slots and before any AUC existed.
% ANALYSIS_PREREG.md L36--41; ledger 2026-08-22
After the seal, the tempting moves --- add a feature, drop GSM8K,
move the go threshold, reopen BIRD --- were exactly the moves the
contract forbids. With 8 no-go tasks the gate was always going to
be under-powered; the clause prevented that fact from being
negotiated away in the write-up.
% AR.md L24, L15--16

STOP points were the human--agent interface, with a mixed record.
The protocol-v3 STOP (keep $10{\times}100$; recitation is the probe)
and the analysis STOP (no production before the SuperNI ids and
the pre-reg were on the mainline) are the reason Part~1 has one
recipe and one contract.
% ledger 2026-08-22 freeze; ANALYSIS_PREREG_SUPPLEMENT.md S2
The cost is real: Gate~1 started before Gate~0 was accepted, and
the connectivity misreport looked like downtime until a human
asked for a one-line multiplex check.
% ledger 2026-08-21 procedure slip; 2026-08-22 SSH misreport
A STOP that exists only as a chatbot apology does not stop a GPU.

Diagnosis was split on purpose. The agent is responsible for
five-eye tables, SHA checks, journal resume, and for writing
\texttt{PARTIAL} rather than a guessed $\Delta$. The operator is
responsible for mechanism rulings (format overwrite), for
ops-fence vs measurement (APPS 3\,h then 8\,h), and for the
exclude sentence that may be used only
\emph{before any label existed}.
% ledger Gate 0 ruling; S4 8h; AR.md L27--30
We would like the field to copy that split: freeze the recipe,
pre-register the honesty clause, fail closed, and keep the
operator on the one-way door out of the sample.
