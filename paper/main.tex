\begin{abstract}
% Operator-owned; drafted after ANALYSIS_RESULTS_v2. Please rewrite.
Fine-tuning a small specialist is cheap, but only after one
decides which tasks to train. Under one frozen protocol
(Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, fixed LoRA, greedy pass@1) we pair a
ten-example probe with a full fine-tune on 61 tasks (53/61 go,
87\% in this regime).
% AR.md L18, L24
The probe \emph{ranks} gain (Spearman $0.755$, CI $[0.595,0.864]$)
and a budget of $K{=}20$ full slots chosen by $\Delta_{\mathrm{pilot}}$
captures 72\% of all positive-gain mass (random 33\%, oracle 74\%).
% AR.md L16, L48; v2 L64
The registered binary LOTO, which included a post-decision
feature, is negative (AUC $0.755$, CI $[0.500,0.929]$ covers 0.5).
% AR.md L15, L36
After removing leakage the revision LOTO is $0.844$
$[0.736,0.939]$ (permutation $p{=}0.001$), but a perfect gate
saves only 2.4\% of positive-gain mass versus always forging.
% v2 L14--16, L55
Instruction embeddings are chance (AUC $0.51$); 23/61 tasks have
base pass@1 $=0$ (format unlock). The table was produced by an
autonomous discover-pilot-decide-train-validate harness.
% v2 L41, L56
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fine-tuning, evaluation, small language models
