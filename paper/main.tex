\begin{abstract}
Fine-tuning a small specialist model is the default path to
cheap task-specific capability, yet the upstream decision --- is
this task worth fine-tuning at all? --- is still made by intuition
or by paying for the full training run. We ask whether ten
labeled examples can answer it in advance. Under a single frozen
protocol (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, fixed LoRA recipe and budgets,
greedy decoding, execution-based verifiers), we measure 61 tasks
% AR.md L24 n=61
--- 11 anchored in the small-model post-training literature and 50
drawn by a pre-registered filter from Super-NaturalInstructions ---
pairing a ten-example pilot with a full fine-tune whose measured
gain serves as ground truth. All analyses were pre-registered.
We find: (1) fine-tuning helps far more often than not --- 53 of
61 tasks (87\%) show positive full-training gains, making ``forge''
% AR.md L18 87% / 86.9%; L24 53 go / 61; abstract freeze keeps 87\%
the empirical default in this regime; (2) pilot signals
significantly rank gain magnitude (Spearman 0.755, 95\% CI
[0.60, 0.86]), enabling budget allocation by ranking;
% AR.md L16,L48 Spearman 0.755 [0.595, 0.864]; abstract freeze rounds CI to [0.60, 0.86]
(3) the
pre-registered binary go/no-go endpoint is not established
(leave-one-task-out AUC 0.755, bootstrap CI [0.50, 0.93]), a
% AR.md L15,L36 AUC 0.755 [0.500, 0.929]; abstract freeze rounds CI to [0.50, 0.93]
power limit rooted in the empirical scarcity of no-go tasks
itself; and (4) description-only features predict below chance
(AUC 0.31) --- the decision-relevant information exists only in
% AR.md L17,L44 text-only 0.309 [0.045, 0.622]; abstract freeze rounds to 0.31
actually running the pilot. We characterize the no-go tail,
including negative transfer on heavily post-trained bases
(GSM8K $-0.23$ despite in-domain training), and release the
% AR.md L58 gsm8k Δ_full −0.230; abstract freeze rounds to −0.23
benchmark, protocol, and all sealed artifacts.
These sealed measurements and pre-registered analyses were produced by an autonomous discover-pilot-decide-train-validate system; without that system's contribution the 61-task ground-truth table would not exist.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} fine-tuning, evaluation, small language models
